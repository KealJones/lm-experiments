Small model lab

Can a small model learn to ask what you need before it answers?

Ask Qwen3 0.6B for a birthday gift idea and it lists generic gifts. It never asks who the gift is for, or why she stopped gardening. So I trained a small patch on 6,000 example conversations where the helper asks first, follows up on the odd detail, looks things up, and checks its answer against what it learned.

It learned the script, not the habit. On the kinds of request it trained on, it went from 0% to 100%, mostly by reciting the training conversations word for word. On a weekend trip, which it never saw, it still asks first. But it asks "What is your age?", gets "idk what you mean lol", and gives up.

    Same harness, same simulated person. The untouched model got two worked examples in its prompt. The patched one got none. In the "everything given" requests, the message already has what's needed, so asking there is a mistake.

    What it says

    • Asking first transferred. The patched model asks on every request that needs it, including the trip it never trained on. It also correctly doesn't ask when the message already says everything.
    • What to ask didn't transfer. It asks in all 30 trip conversations, and none of them find out who's coming or why. The three saved ones all open with "What is your age?"
    • On the trained kinds it's reciting. The saved patched conversations are the same text as the training-style conversation for the same request, and are identical character for character.
    • The training data made that the cheapest thing to learn: 6,000 conversations from a handful of templates with near-identical wording. And a simulated person who matches keywords rewards saying the right words, not asking the right thing.
