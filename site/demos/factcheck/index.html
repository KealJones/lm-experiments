Small model lab

Can a small model catch its own mistakes?

Small models get facts wrong. So let one answer, then start a fresh chat with the same model, hand it the question and its own answer plus Wikipedia and Wikidata tools, and ask it to check. If the second pass works, a phone-sized model could get one message right. 12 questions, several of them "latest" facts that changed after training, and four models from 0.6B to 1.5B parameters.

Not by reviewing itself. The reviewer follows the prompt, not the facts: asked plainly, Qwen3 0.6B approves almost everything. Told to be strict, it rejects almost everything, right answers included. What does work is giving it the right paragraph to read. With the answer in front of it, it was right 21 times out of 21.

What the second pass does

  
The same model reviews each of its own 36 drafts twice, once per prompt. "Final right" is the
    answer the user would get after the review. "False alarms" are right drafts the reviewer called wrong. "Planted"
    is a known-wrong answer per question, like "The capital of Australia is Sydney", reviewed 3 times each (only some
    arms ran it). The row you're looking at above is marked.

  






  
Hand it the paragraph

  
In the "read" arm the code searched Wikipedia for the question and put the top page's matching
    paragraphs in the chat before the model said anything. That simple search found the answer for most questions but
    not all. It returned Emma Stone's biography instead of her films, and the page for Mount Meru instead of
    Kilimanjaro. Split the drafts by whether the answer was actually in that text:

  

  
The two Qwen models read fine: given the answer, they never missed. What they can't do is find it. In the arm
    where they had to search, every Qwen draft made exactly one search and answered from the snippets, which rarely
    contain the answer. None of the small models ever opened a page to look.






  
What it says

  
    
    
  • At 0.6–0.8B, the model can read but it can't search. Give Qwen3 0.6B a paragraph with the answer and it's
      right every time. Ask it to find that paragraph and mostly it doesn't.
    • 
    
  • So improve the tool, not the review prompt. Code should do the searching and choose what to read, and tools
      should return the answer itself, like Wikidata's current office holder or a filmography sorted by date, not a
      page about the subject.
    • 
    
  • Self-review by the same small model doesn't work. A plain review approves, a strict review rejects, and
      neither tracks whether the answer is right. A better second pass is a mechanical check: is the answer actually
      in the retrieved text?
    • 
    
  • The only times review helped were when the drafts were terrible. Qwen2.5 Coder 1.5B went from 8% to 42%
      (free arm, plain review), because its reviewer used tools its drafter hadn't. It still ended below Qwen3's
      drafts.
    •
