Small model lab

Can a tiny model keep track of where it is?

To plan, you have to remember where you are, what you're holding and which doors are open. A plain 3.6M-parameter GPT has nowhere to keep that, so it works it out again from the whole plan at every word. What if it got 8 small memory slots that update as it reads?

The slots helped, most of all when the input got messy. Strip every period out of the problem and the plain model solved 0% of long plans. With slots updated at every word it solved 72%, or 64% with no teaching at all. Bookkeeping mistakes fell from 289 to 61–80.

    Failures across all seeds on the clean tests. Bookkeeping failures are the ones a state table should prevent: going where you already are, taking something that isn't there, opening a door that isn't closed, saying something false.

    Do the taught slots still hold the state?

    Teaching fades out between 50% and 80% of training. Afterwards, a readout checks the slots against the true state on gold plans. Farm and space are skins the model saw only as loose sentences, never as plans.

    The slots kept the state in the skins the model planned in, even after teaching stopped. In farm and space they read the place and the goal item at about chance. The table is tied to the words it learned on, not to the shape of the problem.

    What it says

    • A small carried state helps a small planner. On clean problems the gain is modest (76% → 94–97% on long plans), with far fewer bookkeeping mistakes.
    • When to update matters more than what's taught. Updating only at sentence ends, the slots barely help on messy input (15%), because the periods that trigger updates are gone. Updating at every word: 72%.
    • The simulator teacher isn't needed. Slots trained only to predict which kinds of words come next (self-every) did about as well as the taught ones.
    • It doesn't transfer to new nouns. The taught slots read the place and goal at chance in skins the model never planned in.
    • An earlier run (registers, run 1) found the same direction with sentence-end slots: bookkeeping errors fell from 226 to 38. Its seeds were noisy, which is why v2 used five.

    Next: a save game for a borrowed model

    If trained slots hold state, can they be saved and restored? A quick check on stock Qwen3.5 0.8B said a pretrained model's own recurrent state can't do it: told "the secret word is pineapple", it recalled the word 10 of 10 times with the text in the prompt and 0 of 10 with only its saved state. Register resume plans to train slots onto a real model and find out. It's a plan; nothing has run yet.
