That's Pearl's Canyon Diner. The old guy at the pump said it's just off the main road in Hanksville, so you shouldn't need to hunt for it.

The trick is timing and the order. Get there before 8am, because he said they sell out. Then ask for the burnt end hash. It isn't on the menu, and according to him they only make it for people who ask for it by name.

I can't check hours or whether they're still open from here, so it's worth a quick look before you plan around it. The Hanksville stop falls on the Moab to Capitol Reef leg, so an early breakfast there would fit the drive.
