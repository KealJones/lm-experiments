But it does have a couple of interesting connections:

- **Steve Biko was born the same day.** The South African anti-apartheid activist and founder of the Black Consciousness Movement was born on December 18, 1946, in the Eastern Cape. He died in police custody in 1977, and his death became a turning point in international opposition to apartheid. The 1987 film Cry Freedom is about him.
- **It was the eve of the First Indochina War.** Tensions between French forces and the Viet Minh in Hanoi were coming to a head that week. The French made demands for control of security in the city around that time, and on December 19, the next day, the Viet Minh launched the attack in Hanoi that's usually treated as the start of the war.
