That was **Pearl's Canyon Diner** in Hanksville. The old guy at the pump said it's just off the main road in town.

The trick has two parts:
1. **Get there before 8am**, because they sell out.
2. **Ask for the burnt end hash.** It's not on the menu, and they only make it if you ask for it.

Don't mix it up with Dario's pick. The cash-only spot with the biscuits and gravy is the Chuckwagon Cafe in Page. Nobody said anything about going early there.

It should fit your route easily. You're already doing Moab to Hanksville to Capitol Reef, so if you spend the night in or near Hanksville, you can hit Pearl's first thing and then drive on toward Torrey and the Gifford pies.
