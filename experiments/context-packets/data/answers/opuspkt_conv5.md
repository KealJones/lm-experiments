That's **Pearl's Canyon Diner** in Hanksville, just off the main road in town, the place the old guy in the trucker hat told you about at the pump.

His trick had two parts:
1. **Get there before 8am.** They sell out.
2. **Ask for the burnt end hash.** It's not on the menu. They only make it if you ask for it.

That was just one guy's tip, though, and I haven't checked that the place is real or what its hours are. Look up their current hours before you plan a morning around it.

It's a good fit for the drive from Moab to Capitol Reef, since you'd roll through Hanksville early anyway. And gas is sparse out there on 24, so top off while you're in town.
