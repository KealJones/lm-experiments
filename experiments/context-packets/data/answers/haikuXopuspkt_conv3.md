Okay, here's a menu that's impressive without being a nightmare, and it works for everyone, Marcus's nut allergy included.

**Menu**
- **Starters:** Charcuterie and cheese board with salami, cheeses, fruit, label-checked crackers, fig or apricot jam, and a small bowl of olives. Skip the mixed nuts and the spicy pepper jelly.
- **Salad:** Green salad with a nut-free vinaigrette (lemon, olive oil, mustard, salt). Top it with parsley or basil, not cilantro, for Dana.
- **Garlic bread**
- **Mains:** Vegetarian baked ziti with a mild tomato-basil sauce and mozzarella, plus lemon-herb roast chicken on the side. Keep the pepper light so Tomas is fine. Make the ziti with plain pasta and skip the sausage.
- **Veg tray (optional):** Roasted vegetables with olive oil, salt and herbs. Vegetables are a safe bet for Priya and everyone else.
- **Dessert:** Chocolate pots de creme, made with nut-free chocolate. They're gelatin-free, so Priya can eat them. Lemon olive oil cake with berries is a nice extra. Make it Friday.
- **Drinks:** 3 or 4 bottles of wine (Dana is bringing some), sparkling water with citrus, NA cider, and iced tea or lemonade. Leave out spicy ginger beer.

**Allergy rules for Marcus**
- Clean the boards, knives and counters before you start, and keep any nuts out of the kitchen.
- Read the labels on crackers, chocolate, and flour. No almond flour, and no orgeat or amaretto.
- Skip classic pesto. Ask Marcus about coconut before you use it.

**Shopping list (Friday)**
- Ziti, mozzarella, tomatoes, basil, garlic
- Chicken thighs or breasts, lemons, rosemary or thyme
- Salad greens, parsley, mustard, olive oil, lemons
- Vegetables for the roast tray
- Nut-free dark chocolate, cream, eggs, sugar, lemon for the pots de creme
- Plain flour, butter, eggs, and berries for the cake
- Salami, cheeses, fruit, crackers, fig or apricot jam, a small olive bowl
- Vegetable stock (not chicken stock, since Priya is vegetarian), wine, sparkling water, citrus, NA cider, iced tea

**Timeline**
- Friday: Shop, make the pots de creme and the olive oil cake, and set up the starter board.
- Saturday afternoon: Assemble the ziti and the chicken, and roast the vegetables. Make the garlic bread and salad dressing. Put the ziti in the oven about an hour before 7:45. Roast the chicken on a separate tray.

That's enough to host without losing your mind. Good luck on Saturday.
