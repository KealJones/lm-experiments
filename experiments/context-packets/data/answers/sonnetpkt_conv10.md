Here's a day-by-day plan for you and Maya in Kyoto, April 12–16, 2027. It uses the pacing we agreed on: one main sight in the morning, a long lunch, rest at the hotel in the early afternoon, and something light in the evening. I've assumed one hotel near Kyoto Station for all four nights.

**Before you go**
- Book airline wheelchair assistance for both flights.
- Reserve a wheelchair space on the Haruka from KIX to Kyoto.
- When you email the hotel about step-free access, the doorway, the bathroom and bed height, also ask them to book an accessible (universal design) taxi for **6:30am on Tue Apr 13** to Kiyomizu-dera. Ask that it drop you at the entrance closest to the gate.
- Check Kiyomizu-dera's current accessibility info. Phone ahead to the kaiseki place about a step-free entrance and table seating.

---

**Day 1 – Mon Apr 12: Arrive and settle in**
- KIX staff help you off the plane. Take the Haruka to Kyoto Station, check in and rest.
- At the station: get ICOCA cards and take out some cash at a 7-Eleven ATM.
- Evening: an easy dinner at Kyoto Station or near the hotel. The station has elevators everywhere. If you land early and feel okay, Shosei-en garden is close by, but don't push it.

**Day 2 – Tue Apr 13: Kiyomizu-dera**
- 6:30am taxi to Kiyomizu-dera. Jet lag should make the early start easier on your first morning. Entry is about 500 yen each; ask whether there's a disability discount.
- Avoid Chawan-zaka and Matsubara-dori. They're steep and cobbled, so take a taxi both ways.
- Late breakfast or a long lunch, then back to the hotel to rest.
- Evening: Fushimi Inari, lower torii gates only. It's about 5 minutes on the JR Nara line from Kyoto Station, it's free, and it's nice in the early evening.

**Day 3 – Wed Apr 14: Gardens day (Maya's day)**
- Morning: Kyoto Botanical Garden. It's flat and on the subway, and every station has elevators. Take it slow and use the benches.
- Long lunch in the area, then rest at the hotel.
- Evening: keep it light. Have dinner near the hotel, or visit Shosei-en if you skipped it on Day 1.

**Day 4 – Thu Apr 15: Nishiki Market and kaiseki lunch**
- Mid-morning: a short visit to Nishiki Market. On a weekday it's less crowded, but the aisles are still narrow, so plan on 45 minutes or so, not a full browse.
- **Kaiseki lunch** as your last-full-day treat, about 4,000–6,000 yen per person.
- Rest in the afternoon. In the evening, have a simple dinner and pack.

**Day 5 – Fri Apr 16: Depart**
- If your flight is late, visit Shosei-en or do some last shopping at the station in the morning. If it's early, go straight to the airport.
- Haruka back to KIX (reserve the wheelchair space again). Leave plenty of buffer time for assistance at the airport.

---

**Swaps if you want them**
- **Nara instead of Day 3:** Nara Park is flat and Todai-ji is fairly accessible. It's about 45 minutes on Kintetsu or about an hour on JR. You'd lose the Botanical Garden, though, and that was one of Maya's top picks.
- **Other mornings you could swap in:** Imperial Palace Park (flat), the Arashiyama bamboo grove (mostly flat), and Nijo Castle. The Heian Shrine garden has a lot of gravel, so I'd put it last.

**Budget:** Most days should come in well under $250. Day 2 (taxi plus admissions) and Day 4 (kaiseki) cost the most, but both should still fit.

One thing to decide: if you'd rather have a buffer day before the 6:30am start, move Kiyomizu-dera to Day 3 and the Botanical Garden to Day 2. The downside is that you'd likely be up early anyway from jet lag. Want me to change anything once you know your flight times?
