Your menu is basically set already, so here's the version that works for everyone on the list.

**Starter: charcuterie, no nuts and no pepper jelly**
Salami for everyone except Priya, plus cheeses, sliced fruit, and crackers. Use a mild fig or apricot jam instead of the spicy pepper jelly, and keep a small bowl of olives out. Many hard cheeses use animal rennet, so pick ones labeled vegetarian for Priya. Put the mixed nuts away for the night.

**Main: all-veg baked ziti plus lemon herb roast chicken on the side**
Make the ziti with a mild tomato and basil sauce and mozzarella, no sausage. If you use a jarred sauce, check the label for meat and nuts. Roast the chicken on its own tray with lemon, garlic, and herbs. Skip pesto, since it's usually made with pine nuts or walnuts.

**Sides: green salad, garlic bread, and roasted vegetables**
Garlic is fine, since Brent and Carol aren't coming. Make sure the salad vinaigrette and the bread are nut-free. The roasted vegetables are a good plate for Priya too.

**Dessert: chocolate pots de creme plus a lemon olive oil cake**
Make both on Friday night. Check the chocolate label for nut processing and use plain wheat flour in the cake. Both are vegetarian and spice-free.

**Drinks:** Dana's bottles plus about two of yours, one white and one red. Offer sparkling water with citrus, lemonade, or sparkling cider for the non-drinkers. No orgeat or amaretto anywhere.

**A correction:** Earlier I said coconut usually isn't a problem for tree nut allergies. That was wrong. In the US, coconut is labeled as a tree nut, and some people with tree nut allergies react to it. Skip coconut milk and anything with coconut for Marcus.

**Saturday, in order**
- Friday: make the pots de creme and the lemon cake, chop vegetables, and make the ziti sauce.
- Saturday afternoon: assemble the ziti, prep the chicken, and set the table by 5.
- 7:00: guests arrive. Bake the ziti and roast the chicken so both come out around 7:45.
