Start with the monthly revenue of $33,320.00 and subtract the overall shipping total of $3,025.00:

33,320.00 − 3,025.00 = 30,295.00

Then subtract the sales tax amount of $2,748.90:

30,295.00 − 2,748.90 = 27,546.10

What is left is **$27,546.10**.

In the spreadsheet, that would be =B3-B12-B14 (or whatever cells hold revenue, shipping total, and sales tax amount).

One thing to keep in mind: the $33,320 came from your 24.50 price, and we calculated the tax *on top of* that figure. If you charge customers tax on top of the 24.50 at checkout, the tax was never part of the $33,320, so subtracting it here would understate what you kept. In that case, revenue minus shipping ($30,295.00) is the more accurate number. Subtracting the tax only makes sense if your 24.50 price already includes tax.
