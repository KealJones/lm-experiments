Honest answer: December 18, 1946 isn't one of those big landmark dates, and I can't point to a major world event I'm confident happened on that exact day. I'd rather not make one up.

The best fact for that date is a shared birthday. Steve Biko, the South African anti-apartheid activist who led the Black Consciousness Movement, was also born on December 18, 1946. He died in police custody in 1977, and his death became one of the defining stories of the struggle against apartheid.

For timing, the First Indochina War began the next day, December 19, 1946, when Viet Minh forces attacked French positions in Hanoi. That war led into what became the Vietnam War. More broadly, Spielberg was born in the first year of the postwar baby boom, so he's an early boomer. That generation grew up on TV and Saturday matinees, and a lot of them ended up being the audience for Jaws.
