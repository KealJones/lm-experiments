Don't sign it yet. Riverside is probably the best place for cooking, but it breaks one of your own hard rules. The bedrooms have wall-to-wall carpet, and with your allergies that's the thing you said you couldn't live with. Steam cleaning won't fix that, so "they'll clean it" doesn't solve the problem.

Friday doesn't have to mean yes or no on the whole lease. Before you put down the $200 holding deposit, ask:

- Will they pull the carpet and put in hard flooring before Dec 1? If they say yes, get it in the lease.
- Is the holding deposit refundable if you walk away? Get that answer in writing.
- What do the electric bills usually run? Those floor-to-ceiling windows can push them up.
- Can they waive or lower the $40 amenity fee?

If they won't touch the carpet, the choice is Riverside with carpeted bedrooms or Maple Street. Maple has hardwood throughout, parking included, and it's $1700. The downside is a small galley kitchen and no dishwasher, but you already have a rolling cart that helps with that. You also had a second showing there the next day, so you could see it before Friday.

Score both against your written list (rent cap, no ground floor, no carpet). Riverside fails one line. If the landlord fixes the carpet, it wins. If not, the kitchen has to be worth breaking your own rule for.
