Here's a day-by-day plan for Kyoto, April 12–16, 2027. It follows the pacing we settled on: one main sight in the morning, a long lunch, a rest at the hotel in early afternoon, and something easy in the evening.

**Before you go**
- Ask the airline for wheelchair assistance at both ends.
- Email the hotel to confirm the doorway width, bathroom setup and bed height.
- Book the accessible taxi for Kiyomizu-dera (6:30 pickup on Tue Apr 13). Ask for the drop-off closest to the gate.
- Call the kaiseki restaurant to confirm step-free entry and Western-style seating.

---

**Day 1 – Mon, Apr 12: Arrive**
- Land at Kansai Airport with wheelchair assistance, then take the Haruka express to Kyoto Station.
- Buy ICOCA cards at the station and take out 20,000–30,000 yen in cash.
- Check in and look over the room.
- Evening: a short, easy dinner in the Kyoto Station building, then an early night. Jet lag will help with tomorrow's early start.

**Day 2 – Tue, Apr 13: Kiyomizu-dera**
- 6:30: accessible taxi to Kiyomizu-dera, arriving before the crowds. The approach is steep and cobbled, so take it slowly and use the closest drop-off.
- Late morning: breakfast or brunch nearby, then a long lunch.
- Early afternoon: rest at the hotel.
- Late afternoon (optional): Shosei-en garden, a short ride from the station. It's quiet and flat. Check its closing time first.
- Evening: dinner near the hotel.

**Day 3 – Wed, Apr 14: Nishiki Market + Fushimi Inari**
- Morning: Nishiki Market. Weekday mornings are the only workable time because the aisles get narrow and packed later.
- Long lunch in the Shijo area.
- Early afternoon: rest at the hotel.
- Evening: Fushimi Inari, lower gates only. It's about 5 minutes on the JR Nara line and free. Evenings there are calm and the gates are lit.

**Day 4 – Thu, Apr 15: Gardens + farewell kaiseki**
- Morning: Kyoto Botanical Garden, picked for Maya. The paths are wide and there are plenty of benches.
- Lunch: the farewell kaiseki, about 4,000–6,000 yen per person. We planned it as a lunch kaiseki. If you'd rather have a dinner one, expect higher prices.
- Early afternoon: rest at the hotel.
- Evening: a light dinner and packing.

**Day 5 – Fri, Apr 16: Depart**
- If the flight leaves in the afternoon, there's time for a short final stop near the station, such as Shosei-en if you skipped it on Day 2.
- Take the Haruka back to Kansai Airport. Leave enough time for the train plus the usual 2–3 hour check-in window for an international flight.

---

**Swap options if energy or weather changes the plan**
- **Arashiyama bamboo grove** (the main path is flat) instead of Nishiki/Fushimi on Day 3. Go early for the same reason as Kiyomizu.
- **Nara day trip** instead of Day 3: about 45 minutes on Kintetsu or about an hour on JR. It's a bigger day, so keep the evening empty.
- **Nijo Castle** or **Kyoto Imperial Palace Park** in place of the Botanical Garden.

**Budget check:** $250 a day for two easily covers this. The most expensive day is Day 4 because of the kaiseki, and the other days are cheap.

**Please double-check these:** the Kiyomizu-dera fee (around 500 yen, possibly lower for wheelchair users), its opening time (around 6:00) and the taxi fare (around 2,000–2,500 yen) are rough estimates. Shosei-en and Botanical Garden hours need checking too.
