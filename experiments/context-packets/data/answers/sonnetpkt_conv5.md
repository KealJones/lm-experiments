Pearl's Canyon Diner, in Hanksville. The old guy said it's just off the main road in town. (You wrote it on your hand, so I'm guessing the hand's been washed since, lol.)

The trick has two parts:
- **Get there before 8am.** He said they sell out.
- **Ask for the "burnt end hash."** It's not on the menu, and the kitchen only makes it if you ask for it.

Heads up: all of that is just what the gas station dude told you. I haven't checked that the place exists or what its hours are. You're going in April, and small-town spots out there often run reduced or seasonal hours then, so give them a call or look them up a week or two before you go. Otherwise you could show up at 7:30 and find it closed.

It lines up well with your route, though. Hanksville is right between Moab and Torrey/Capitol Reef, so an early breakfast there on the Moab-to-Torrey day works fine. Fill up while you're in town too, since Highway 24 has long gaps between gas stations.
