**Where it could actually go wrong (mostly Marcus):**
- **Salami:** mortadella and some fancy salamis have pistachios in them, and a lot of them (soppressata, pepperoni, "calabrese") are spicy, which is a problem for Tomas. Get a plain Genoa or similar and read the label.
- **Chocolate, crackers, jam, bread:** read every label for "tree nuts" *and* "may contain / processed in a facility with tree nuts." Specialty chocolate is the most likely one to have that warning. Some fig jams have walnuts in them too.
- **No pesto, no nut garnishes, no candied nuts on the salad.** Pine nuts count as a no for tonight.
- **Kitchen:** no nuts in the house this week, clean boards, and a separate serving spoon for each dish.
- **Dana:** no cilantro anywhere. Nothing on this menu needs it, so you're good.
- **Tomas:** no chili flakes in the marinara or on the chicken. Go light on the black pepper too.
- **Priya:** the ziti is her main. If you want to go all the way, real Parmigiano-Reggiano is made with animal rennet, and some vegetarians care about that. Grab a parm labeled vegetarian or ask her.
