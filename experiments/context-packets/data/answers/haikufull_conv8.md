Honestly, I wouldn't sign it just to be done. Riverside has a problem that's on your own list: the bedrooms have wall-to-wall carpet, and you said no carpet at all because your allergies are bad. A steam clean doesn't deal with that the way replacing the carpet would. So right now Riverside fails one of your own filters, and it's the one about your health.

Before Friday, I'd text or email the landlord and ask for these in writing:

- Bedroom carpet replaced with hard flooring before December 1, or a credit so you can do it yourself. If she says no, you know where you stand.
- Whether the $200 holding deposit is refundable and whether it goes toward first month's rent.
- The $40 amenity fee waived for the first few months, which you already liked as an idea.
- The $100 elevator deposit for move-in day, confirmed in the lease.

If she agrees to replace the carpet in writing, Riverside is a real contender and the kitchen is a strong reason to pick it. If she won't, you're choosing between a place with carpet you already know will bother you and Maple, which has hardwood, lower rent, and a kitchen you can improve with that rolling cart. Maple has another showing tomorrow, so ask her to hold it for 24 hours or take an application tonight.

Also, Tyler will be annoyed about the south-facing windows. That's his to deal with, but tell him now so it isn't a surprise on move-in day.

Before you sign anything, read the 12-month lease for the deposit terms, the fee schedule, and the carpet promise. A verbal promise from the tour doesn't count unless it's written into the lease.
