Ha, okay, here's the final menu. Everything below works for all 8 of you, so nobody gets a special plate.

**The menu**

- **Starter board:** salami, the cheeses, fruit, crackers you've checked the labels on, fig or apricot jam, and a small bowl of olives on the side. The mixed nuts and pepper jelly stay put away.
- **Main:** vegetarian baked ziti with a mild tomato-basil sauce and mozzarella. No sausage, no chili flakes, and only a little black pepper.
- **Meat side:** lemon-herb roast chicken (lemon, garlic, thyme or rosemary, olive oil, salt, no heavy pepper). Cook it on its own pan with its own tools so the ziti stays fully veg for Priya.
- **Sides:** green salad with a plain lemon or red wine vinaigrette, garlic bread, and roasted veg if you've got the energy (zucchini, peppers, red onion). If not, skip the veg. Nobody will notice.
- **Dessert:** chocolate pots de crème, made Friday. Make the lemon olive oil cake only if you're feeling good. One great dessert beats two stressed ones.
- **Drinks:** 3–4 bottles of wine plus Dana's, sparkling water with citrus, NA cider, and iced tea or lemonade. Tomas will be covered.

**The things that'll actually bite you**

- **Marcus (nuts):** check the labels on the chocolate, the crackers, the bread for the garlic bread, and any store-bought dressing. Wipe the counters and use clean boards. No pesto, no almond anything, no amaretto. Skipping coconut is easier than asking him.
- **Dana (cilantro):** keep it out completely. Use basil and parsley for garnish.
- **Priya (veg):** don't use Worcestershire in the dressing, since it has anchovies. If you want to be extra careful, real Parmigiano-Reggiano is made with animal rennet. Mozzarella on the ziti plus a "vegetarian parmesan" label from the store covers you.
- **Tomas (spice):** keep the chili and hot sauce off the table. Put the pepper grinder out so people can add their own.

**Shopping list**

- *Pasta/ziti:* 2 lb ziti or penne, 2 large jars of mild marinara (or 3 cans crushed tomatoes, onion and garlic), 2 lb mozzarella, 15 oz ricotta, vegetarian parm, fresh basil
- *Chicken:* 2 whole chickens or about 4 lb bone-in thighs, 3 lemons, 1 head of garlic, thyme or rosemary
- *Salad and sides:* 2 bags of mixed greens, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red wine vinegar, Dijon, 1–2 baguettes (label-checked), butter, parsley, veg for roasting if you're doing it
- *Pots de crème:* 8 oz nut-free dark chocolate (check the label for "may contain tree nuts"), 2 cups heavy cream, 1 cup milk, 6 egg yolks, sugar, vanilla, plus berries or whipped cream on top
- *Drinks:* wine, sparkling water, NA cider, iced tea or lemonade, limes and lemons
- *Already have:* the board stuff and olive oil
