**Day 1 – Monday, April 12: Arrival**

- Evening: a short visit to Shosei-en garden if you arrive early enough and Maya has the energy, since it is close by. If not, have a simple dinner near the station and go to bed early.

**Day 2 – Tuesday, April 13: Kiyomizu-dera**
- 6:30: The accessible taxi picks you up at the hotel. Ask the driver to drop you at the entrance nearest the temple gate, away from the steep streets of Chawan-zaka and Matsubara-dori.
- 6:45 to about 8:30: Visit the temple before the crowds arrive. Ask staff about the slope route, and at the ticket booth ask about a reduced fee.
- Taxi back for a relaxed breakfast, then rest at the hotel through midday.
- Late afternoon: Shosei-en, if you skipped it on Day 1, or simply a quiet afternoon.
- Evening: dinner near the hotel.

I put Kiyomizu-dera early in the trip on purpose. If it rains or Maya is too tired, you can move it to the morning of Day 4.

**Day 3 – Wednesday, April 14: Nara (optional day trip)**
- Morning: Take the Kintetsu line (about 45 minutes) or the JR Nara line (about an hour) to Nara. Visit Nara Park and the deer, then Todai-ji and the giant Buddha.
- Lunch in Nara, then return to Kyoto in the early afternoon and rest.
- Early evening: Fushimi Inari, which is five minutes from Kyoto Station on the JR Nara line. Stay around the main shrine and the first gates near the base.
- If you would rather not go to Nara, spend a calmer morning at Nijo Castle or along the flat main path at Arashiyama instead.

**Day 4 – Thursday, April 15: Gardens and the special meal**
- Morning: Kyoto Botanical Garden, which Maya should enjoy most, with flat paths and the greenhouse. Take the Karasuma subway line to Kitayama Station.
- Lunch: your kaiseki lunch in the city center. Book a table-seating restaurant and confirm a step-free entrance when you reserve.
- Afternoon: rest at the hotel.
- Evening: a light dinner and packing. If you are both feeling energetic after lunch, you could take a short look at Nishiki Market, though it is narrow and can be busy.

**Day 5 – Friday, April 16: Departure**
- A relaxed breakfast and check-out.
- Take the Haruka to Kansai International Airport, with the wheelchair space reserved, and leave extra time for assistance at the airport.
