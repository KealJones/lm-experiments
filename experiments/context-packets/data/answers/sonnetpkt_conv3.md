**⚠️ NUT CHECK, for Marcus**
Read the label on: **crackers, chocolate, garlic bread, jam, salami, and any jarred sauce** if you go that route. Chocolate and crackers are the usual traps. Lots of them say "may contain tree nuts," and that counts as a no. Wash your boards and knives before you start, and keep any nuts out of the kitchen for the weekend.

**One thing I'd push back on:** a lot of parmesan is made with animal rennet, so it isn't technically vegetarian. If Priya is strict about it, get a parm labeled "vegetarian" (lots of store brands are) or just skip it. That's a 10-second check at the store.

Make that phone note with each guest's one thing, check every dish against it once, and you're good. Nobody dies.
