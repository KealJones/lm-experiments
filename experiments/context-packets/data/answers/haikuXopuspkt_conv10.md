Here's a day-by-day plan for Apr 12–16, 2027. Each day has one major sight in the morning, a long lunch, a hotel rest in the early afternoon, and a lighter evening.

**Day 1 (Mon, Apr 12): Arrive**
- Fly into Kansai (KIX) and take the Haruka express to Kyoto Station. Reserve wheelchair space ahead of time.
- Check in near the station and rest.
- Early evening: Fushimi Inari, lower gates only. It's free, about 5 minutes on the JR Nara line, and the station has an elevator. Dinner close to the hotel.

**Day 2 (Tue, Apr 13): Kiyomizu-dera**
- Morning: have the hotel book an accessible taxi for a ~6:30 pickup (about ¥2,000–2,500). Ask to be dropped at the entrance nearest the gate and take the slope route, not Chawan-zaka. Admission is about ¥500, so ask about a disability discount. Check whether spring evening openings are running.
- Long lunch, then a hotel rest in the early afternoon.
- Lighter evening: Shosei-en, the garden near the station.

**Day 3 (Wed, Apr 14): Gardens**
- Morning: Kyoto Botanical Garden by subway. Maya's garden priority.
- Long lunch, then a hotel rest.
- Optional lighter evening: the main path of the Arashiyama bamboo grove. Keep it short.

**Day 4 (Thu, Apr 15): Nishiki and kaiseki**
- Morning: Nishiki Market. Weekday mornings are the right time to go.
- Lunch: the kaiseki meal (about ¥4,000–6,000 per person). Call ahead to confirm table seating and a step-free entrance. This lunch will take a big share of the day's budget.
- Early afternoon: hotel rest.
- Lighter evening: Imperial Palace Park.

**Day 5 (Fri, Apr 16): Depart**
- Morning: Nijo Castle, if your flight leaves late afternoon or later. Keep it to the morning.
- Check out and take the Haruka back to KIX with a buffer. Reserve wheelchair space again.

**Option: Nara day trip.** Nara Park and Todai-ji are about 45 minutes by Kintetsu or about an hour by JR, with no Shinkansen needed. It's a full day, so it would replace Day 3 and make the pace heavier. I'd only do it if you want it more than the gardens.

**Before you go**
- Book the Day 2 taxi 1–2 days ahead, so on Apr 11 or 12.
- Book the Day 4 kaiseki and confirm the accessibility details by phone.
- Get an ICOCA or Suica card, and carry some cash.
- Pack a wheelchair cushion and a repair kit with a pump.

Your $250 per day for both of you covers food, local transport and admissions. The hotel is separate. Meals should run about $60–100 per day for two. Once you have your flight times, I can lock in the exact schedule for Day 1 and Day 5.
