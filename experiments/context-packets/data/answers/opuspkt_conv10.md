Here's a day-by-day plan for Kyoto, Apr 12–16, built on the rhythm we settled on: one big thing in the morning, a long lunch, a rest at the hotel, then something light in the evening. Everything assumes one base near Kyoto Station.

**Before you go**
- Email the hotel now and ask them to book an accessible taxi for **Tue Apr 13, ~6:30am** to Kiyomizu-dera. You land the day before, so you can't book it 1–2 days ahead from Kyoto.
- Reserve wheelchair space on the Haruka for both directions.
- Call or email ahead to book the **Thu Apr 15 kaiseki lunch**. Confirm table seating (no tatami) and a step-free entrance.
- Pack: Maya's cushion, the repair kit/pump, and some yen in cash.

**Mon Apr 12 (arrival)**
- KIX → Haruka to Kyoto Station. Pick up ICOCA cards at the airport.
- Check in and rest. Don't plan anything else.
- Evening: an easy dinner in or near Kyoto Station, which is flat with elevators everywhere. Double-check tomorrow's taxi with the front desk.

**Tue Apr 13 (Kiyomizu-dera)**
- 6:30 taxi to the entrance closest to the gate, then the slope route up. Skip Chawan-zaka. Admission is about ¥500; ask whether there's a disability discount.
- Late breakfast or early long lunch nearby, then taxi back.
- Early afternoon: hotel rest. You'll need it after the early start.
- Early evening: **Fushimi Inari, lower gates only.** It's 5 minutes on the JR Nara line, the station has an elevator, and entry is free. Turn back wherever the path starts to climb.

**Wed Apr 14 (garden day for Maya)**
- Morning: **Kyoto Botanical Garden** by subway. It's big and flat with lots of benches. Take it slow.
- Long lunch near the garden or back toward Karasuma.
- Hotel rest.
- Late afternoon: **Shosei-en**, a short trip from the station. It's small and calm, a good gentle second garden. Check its closing time.
- *Nara swap:* If you want the Nara day trip, use this day for it. Kintetsu takes about 45 minutes. Do Nara Park and Todai-ji in the morning, have lunch there, and come back to rest. Move the Botanical Garden to Thursday morning instead of Nishiki.

**Thu Apr 15 (last full day + special lunch)**
- Morning: **Nishiki Market** right around opening, while it's a weekday and still quiet. It's narrow, so go early, before it fills up.
- **Kaiseki lunch** (~¥4,000–6,000 each). Let this be the main event of the day.
- Hotel rest.
- Evening: a slow roll through **Imperial Palace Park**. Or, if Kiyomizu has a spring evening opening that week, go back for the night views.

**Fri Apr 16 (departure)**
- Depending on your flight time: a relaxed breakfast, packing, and maybe a quick final wander around the station area.
- Haruka back to KIX. Leave yourselves extra time for wheelchair assistance at the airport.

**Swaps if you want them:** Nijo Castle or the main path of the Arashiyama bamboo grove can replace Wednesday's afternoon or Thursday's morning. I'd add only one, not both. Heian Shrine is lower on the list because of its gravel paths.

**Budget check:** At $250/day, you have plenty of room. Meals at $60–100, plus a taxi or two, transit, and admissions, still leaves slack on most days. Thursday runs highest because of the kaiseki, but it still fits.
