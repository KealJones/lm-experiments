Here's the math, using last month's actual revenue rather than the projection:

1. Monthly revenue: $33,320.00
2. Minus shipping total ($3,025.00): $33,320.00 - $3,025.00 = $30,295.00
3. Minus sales tax ($2,748.90): $30,295.00 - $2,748.90 = $27,546.10

That leaves **$27,546.10**.

One thing to check before you rely on that number: sales tax is normally collected from the customer on top of the price and passed on to the state or local authority, so it usually isn't part of your revenue to begin with. The $2,748.90 was calculated on top of the $33,320 figure, which suggests that revenue number is pre-tax. If so, subtracting the tax takes money out that was never in the revenue, and $27,546.10 understates what's left. If you want a figure for what you actually keep before cost of goods and packaging, $30,295.00 (revenue minus shipping) is the one that matches how the revenue was built.
