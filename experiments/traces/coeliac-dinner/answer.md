If you have a Large Soup Pot have I got a meal for you!
I found this super easy and cozy ONE POT CHICKEN AND RICE MEAL!
It is entirely gluten free BUT be aware it uses garlic and onion which does have Fructans, a type of carbohydrate found in foods like wheat, leeks, asparagus, artichokes, and most notably, onions and garlic, garlic and onions do NOT have Gluten but can cause similar issues just so you are aware.
* https://culinahealth.com/blog/gluten-free-but-still-bloated-garlic-and-onions-may-be-to-blame/
* https://gluten.org/faq/i-thought-i-was-gluten-sensitive-but-lately-ive-been-hearing-that-maybe-fructan-not-gluten-is-what-causes-problems-can-you-explain/

Shopping List:
- butter or vegan butter
- shredded or chopped carrots
- salt and pepper
- long grain white rice (Lundberg White Jasmine Rice recommended)
- dried minced onion
- dried minced garlic
- dried parsley flakes
- ** LOOK FOR GLUTEN-FREE SPECIFICALLY ** chicken stock
  - if you cant find "Gluten-Free" label, review the ingredients and AVOID "Natural Flavoring" and "Yeast Extract" they may contain gluten to an extent.
- 2 chicken breasts

I have confirmed that every ingredient is gluten free, as long as you are sure to check the chicken stock.
